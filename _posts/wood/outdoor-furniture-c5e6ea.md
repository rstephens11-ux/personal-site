I chainsaw milled a Maple that had fallen in my yard and ended up with some usable boards. I was able to make a picnic table and one of the outdoor benches using that Maple..still being used years later today! The other bench I used some bug bitten Oak I had bought from facebook market (not knowing there were bugs) but is also holding up so far. One of these days I'll make some new ones as these aren't my finest work.
