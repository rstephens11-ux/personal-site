The person

The guy at the bench.

Ryan Stephens builds things by hand on Midcoast Maine — furniture with joints cut to outlive their maker, software that doesn't rent itself back to you, and a garden that mostly forgives him. He works within earshot of the tide, which sets the schedule better than any calendar app has managed to.

By daylight: the bench, the beds, the keyboard. By lamplight: “Young George,” an animated series about the most honest man in history, misplaced in time. He believes the two kinds of work are the same work — measure twice, cut once, ship something you'd sign your name to.

He is not on social media. This page is the whole internet presence. It loads fast, tracks nothing, and will still open in a plain text editor in twenty years — which is also the entire point.

“If you can't repair it, you don't own it.”
— the rule for both furniture and software
