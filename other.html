yt-dlp app

macOS app · shell wrapper

Built a native macOS app shell around yt-dlp — the powerful open-source video downloader that handles YouTube and hundreds of other sites. The app wraps the command-line tool in a clean interface so I don't have to remember flags every time. Under the hood it's still yt-dlp doing the heavy lifting; the app just makes it feel like something I actually want to use.
