Blanket Chest - 6 Board Chest

white pine · dovetails · milk paint

I followed a 1999 FWW article by Mike Dunbar for a classic 6 Board Chest. Hand-cut rabbets front and back, a till inside for the small things, and a lid hung on hand-set hinges. — I also bought Sea Beckets from Etsy which look great.

It holds my sons toys for now, and will hopefully hold my great grandsons else's in 2124, which is the entire point of building it this way.

MaterialWhite pine
JoineryHand-cut dovetails
FinishMilk paint + oil
StatusIn service, November 2025
