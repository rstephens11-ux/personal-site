Selected work

Made slowly, made to keep.

Furniture, a cartoon, software, and vegetables. The through-line is ownership — things built well enough that nobody has to rent them back.

01

Blanket Chest

white pine · dovetails · milk paint

The piece that started the shop habit. Hand-cut dovetails front and back, a till inside for the small things, and a lid hung on hand-set hinges. No screws anywhere — the joints are the hardware.

It holds blankets now, and will hold somebody else's in 2124, which is the entire point of building it this way.

MaterialWhite pine
JoineryHand-cut dovetails
FinishMilk paint + oil
StatusIn service, 2024
02

Young George

animated series · in development

George Washington, age 20 — a surveyor, not yet a legend — steps through a mysterious boundary stone in 1752 and lands in present-day Mt. Vernon, New York. Three zoomer roommates take him in. He cannot tell a lie. The century cannot stop.

The satire points at us, not him: George is played straight, horrified that nobody knows their neighbors. Season one is New York; the show's secret is that he goes home and becomes the First President because of what he saw.

FormatAdult animated series
Seasons4 mapped (NY→Philly→DC→VA)
Theme“No Cap”

03

Own-Your-Backups App

desktop software · no subscription

An iMazing alternative built on a radical premise: you buy a tool, and then it's yours. No monthly fee, no device limit, no account required, no phone-home. Your phone's backups live on your machine, in formats you can open.

Currently in the "it compiles, mostly" stage. The spec is one sentence: behave like a good hand plane — does its job, asks nothing, lasts.

TypeDesktop app
PriceOnce, not monthly
DevicesUnlimited
StatusIn progress
04

The Garden

four raised beds · zone 6a

The longest-running project and the only one with an adversary. Four beds of heirlooms against one bold squirrel; the current score stands 14–2 in the gardener's favor, pending appeal.

August is the payoff: most dinners are a tomato eaten standing over the bed, still warm from the sun. 10/10, would feud again.
